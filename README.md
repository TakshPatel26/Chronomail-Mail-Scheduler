1) ChronoMail AI — Autonomous Email Scheduler & Conflict Engine

A minimal, executive-grade web application designed for business club operations and student leaders. It automatically reads incoming emails, extracts schedule events, detects meeting clashes with 1-click resolution, synchronizes schedule updates, and sets proactive contextual reminders.

2) 🌟 Key Business Features

* Autonomous Email Parsing: Natural language extraction of event titles, dates, time ranges, meeting links (Zoom/Google Meet), and physical room locations.
* Clash & Conflict Radar: Evaluates overlapping meeting intervals and flags double-bookings with instant 1-click alternative suggestions (e.g., shifting to immediately after the conflicting meeting).
* Schedule Update & Revision Tracking: Identifies reschedule emails and updates the original event while keeping an audit trail of the old vs new time.
* Contextual Proactive Reminders: Automatically configures 24h flight check-in reminders, 3h airport departures, and 15m/1h meeting preparation alerts.
* Minimalist Executive Interface: High-contrast, clean typography, subtle card boundaries, and distraction-free workspace.
